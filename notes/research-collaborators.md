# Sam Abbott's recent collaborators, 2024 to 2026

Compiled 12 September 2026 for the collaborators slide(s) in the research exchange deck.
Sources: GitHub contributors and 2024 to 2026 commit authors for the packages listed in the brief, DESCRIPTION/Project.toml author fields, paper author lists on disk, and the epinowcast community website.
Ordering within each group runs from most to least collaboration, judged by commit volume since 2024, package authorship role, and paper author position, in that order.
Avatar column: "identicon (confirmed)" means the image was downloaded and is a generated pattern, not a photo.
Other GitHub accounts are marked "not checked" for avatar; a filled name field is not proof of a real photo.
Blank cells mean the fact was not found in local files or the GitHub API, not that it is false.
